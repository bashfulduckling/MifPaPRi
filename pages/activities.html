Activities & Events

Workshops, seminars, and community engagement

Upcoming Events

Research Methods Workshop

📅 April 20-22, 2026 | 📍 Davao City

A three-day intensive workshop on qualitative and mixed-methods research design. Ideal for graduate students and early-career researchers. Limited to 30 participants.

Academic Writing Writeshop

📅 May 10-12, 2026 | 📍 Cagayan de Oro

Develop your academic writing skills in this hands-on writeshop. Bring a draft article or paper for feedback from experienced mentors. Open to all researchers.

Peacebuilding Forum 2026

📅 June 15-16, 2026 | 📍 General Santos City

An annual forum bringing together peacebuilders, researchers, NGOs, and community leaders. Share best practices, network, and discuss challenges in peacebuilding work.

Past Events

Youth Research Seminar Series (2025)

📅 Throughout 2025 | 📍 Multiple Locations

A series of monthly seminars where early-career researchers shared their work on youth, education, and peacebuilding. Topics ranged from student activism to educational policy.

Policy Research Consultation (2024)

📅 October 2024 | 📍 Zamboanga City

Consultation workshop with government officials and policy stakeholders to discuss evidence-based policy recommendations on local governance and development.

Research Capacity Assessment Workshop (2024)

📅 August 2024 | 📍 Cotabato City

Workshop assessing research capacity needs and identifying priorities for capacity building programs in the region.
