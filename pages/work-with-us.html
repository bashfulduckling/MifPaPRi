Work With Us

Partnership and collaboration opportunities

How to Collaborate

The Mindanao Institute welcomes collaboration with researchers, organizations, institutions, and individuals who share our commitment to peace, evidence-based policy, and community development. Here are ways you can work with us:

Research Partnerships

We invite researchers and institutions to collaborate on joint research projects. Whether you have a specific research idea or would like to contribute expertise to one of our ongoing projects, we welcome your involvement. Research partnerships may include joint funding proposals, collaborative publications, and knowledge exchange.

Training and Workshops

Organizations can request tailored training workshops for their teams on research methods, academic writing, peacebuilding, or policy analysis. We also invite organizations to co-host workshops or training events with the Institute.

Mentorship Opportunities

Early-career researchers can apply for mentorship with experienced scholars. Mentors provide guidance on research design, career development, academic publishing, and navigating the research landscape. Mentorship engagements are flexible and customized to individual needs.

Internships and Student Engagement

We offer opportunities for graduate students and advanced undergraduates to intern with the Institute and contribute to research projects. Interns gain practical research experience and exposure to peacebuilding work.

Institutional Partnerships

Academic institutions, NGOs, government agencies, and community organizations can establish formal partnerships with the Institute. Partnerships may include joint programming, resource sharing, collaborative research, and knowledge exchange.

Getting Started

To discuss collaboration opportunities or express interest in working with the Institute, please complete the contact form or email us at info@mindanaoinsitute.org. In your message, please include:
