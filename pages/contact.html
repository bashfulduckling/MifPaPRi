Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to respond to inquiries?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 2-3 business days. During peak periods, it may take up to one week.

How can I request a training workshop?

Please use the contact form above and select "Training Request" as your subject. Include details about your organization, the topic you're interested in, and your preferred dates.

Can I apply for mentorship?

Yes! We offer mentorship to early-career researchers. Contact us through the form above with information about your research interests and career goals.

How can I collaborate with the Institute?

We welcome research partnerships, joint projects, and institutional collaborations. Please visit our Work With Us page for more details, or contact us directly.

Where can I find your publications?

Our publications are available on our Publications page. For specific papers or reports, you can also contact us directly.

Do you offer internships?

Yes, we offer internship opportunities for graduate students and advanced undergraduates. Contact us for current opportunities and application requirements.
