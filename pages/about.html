About Us

Understanding our mission, vision, and commitment

Who We Are

The Mindanao Institute for Peace and Policy Research is a research and policy organization dedicated to advancing knowledge, peace, and development in Mindanao and the broader Southeast Asian region. We bring together researchers, practitioners, and community leaders to tackle the complex challenges facing our society.

Why We Were Established

The Institute was established to address the critical need for rigorous, locally-rooted research on peace, governance, and development in Mindanao. We believe that evidence-based policy and grassroots engagement are essential for building sustainable peace and prosperity.

Our Mission

To advance peace and development in Mindanao through rigorous research, capacity building, and collaborative partnerships that empower researchers and communities to create positive change.

Our Vision

A Mindanao where peace, research, and informed policy-making enable sustainable development, social justice, and improved quality of life for all communities.

Our Guiding Values

  • Integrity: We conduct research with honesty, rigor, and transparency.
  • Inclusivity: We amplify diverse voices and perspectives from Mindanao's communities.
  • Collaboration: We work with partners, scholars, and practitioners to maximize impact.
  • Relevance: We produce research that addresses real-world challenges and informs policy.
  • Empowerment: We support early-career researchers and community leaders.

Based in Mindanao, Southern Philippines

The Mindanao Institute is rooted in the diverse and dynamic context of Mindanao, the second-largest island in the Philippines. This location provides us with unique insights into the region's complex history, cultures, and development challenges. Our presence in Mindanao enables us to build strong relationships with local communities, institutions, and stakeholders.
