Projects & Research

Current and recent research initiatives

Our Research Projects

The Mindanao Institute conducts research on key issues affecting the region. Here are some of our current and recent projects:

Youth Engagement in Peacebuilding

Theme: Youth & Peace | Partners: Local NGOs | Year: 2025-2026

This study examines the role of young people in grassroots peacebuilding initiatives. We explore barriers and opportunities for youth participation in conflict transformation and community development in Mindanao.

Governance and Local Development

Theme: Governance | Partners: Government Agencies | Year: 2024-2026

A multi-year research project analyzing local governance systems and their impact on development outcomes. The study evaluates institutional performance and community participation in local government.

Education for Peace in Mindanao

Theme: Education & Peace | Partners: Schools, Universities | Year: 2025

This research explores how peace education is integrated into school curricula and its effects on student attitudes toward conflict and diversity. We work with schools across Mindanao to understand educational peacebuilding.

Community-Based Resource Management

Theme: Environment & Development | Partners: Community Organizations | Year: 2024-2025

An investigation into community-based approaches to natural resource management and sustainable development. The study documents best practices and lessons learned from local resource management initiatives.

Policy Brief Series: Mindanao Development

Theme: Policy Research | Partners: Policy Institutes | Year: Ongoing

An ongoing series of policy briefs addressing current development challenges in Mindanao. These briefs provide evidence-based recommendations for policymakers and stakeholders on critical issues.

Research Capacity Building Program

Theme: Capacity Building | Partners: Universities, Research Centers | Year: 2024-2026

A comprehensive program to strengthen research capacity among early-career researchers in Mindanao. Through training, mentorship, and collaborative research opportunities, we support the next generation of scholars.
