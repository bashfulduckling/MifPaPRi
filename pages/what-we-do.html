Our Programs

Research and Policy Studies

We conduct rigorous research on peace, governance, and development in Mindanao. Our studies inform policy recommendations and contribute to academic knowledge. Our research areas include peacebuilding, youth engagement, governance, and socio-economic development.

Training Workshops on Research Design and Methods

We offer comprehensive training workshops for researchers at all levels. Topics include qualitative research methods, quantitative analysis, research design, literature review, and ethical research practices. These workshops are designed to build research capacity in Mindanao.

Academic Writing Workshops

Our writeshops focus on helping researchers develop strong academic writing skills. We cover topics such as article structure, clarity and style, peer review preparation, and publication strategies. Participants receive constructive feedback on their work from experienced mentors.

Mentorship and Support for Early-Career Researchers

The Institute provides mentoring relationships between experienced researchers and early-career scholars, particularly those based in Mindanao. Our mentorship programs offer guidance on research design, career development, networking, and navigating academic publishing.

Support and Collaboration with International Scholars

We facilitate collaboration between local Mindanao researchers and international scholars working on related topics. This includes research partnerships, joint publications, exchange programs, and knowledge sharing to strengthen the global research community focused on Mindanao.
