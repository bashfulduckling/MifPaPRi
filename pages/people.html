Our People

Meet our team

Core Team

Dr. Maria Santos

Executive Director

Dr. Santos leads the Institute's strategic direction and research programs. She holds a PhD in Peace and Development Studies and has over 15 years of experience in peacebuilding and policy research in Southeast Asia.

Dr. Ramon Cruz

Research Director

Dr. Cruz oversees all research projects and academic initiatives. His expertise is in governance, local development, and qualitative research methods. He is committed to building research capacity in Mindanao.

Jennifer Lao

Program Coordinator

Jennifer manages day-to-day operations, event planning, and stakeholder engagement. She is passionate about youth development and community partnerships. Jennifer brings 8 years of experience in the NGO sector.

Dr. Ahmad Hassan

Senior Researcher

Dr. Hassan leads research on conflict dynamics and peacebuilding strategies. He has published extensively on Mindanao's peace process and collaborates with international scholars and institutions.

Michael Reyes

Communications Officer

Michael manages the Institute's digital presence, publications, and outreach activities. He works to ensure our research reaches diverse audiences including policymakers, practitioners, and the general public.

Dr. Fatima Abubakar

Mentorship Coordinator

Dr. Abubakar oversees the mentorship program connecting early-career researchers with experienced scholars. She provides guidance on career development, research design, and academic publishing.

Collaborating Researchers

The Institute works with numerous researchers and scholars from academic institutions, NGOs, and government agencies. Our collaborators contribute expertise, insights, and local knowledge to our research initiatives.

If you are interested in collaborating with the Mindanao Institute, please contact us.
